YOUR STARTING POINT

A clearer picture of eligibility.

Requirements differ between categories. These are the main areas to consider before starting an application.

01

Age

Silver, Gold and Platinum currently require the main applicant to be at least 25.

02

Financial commitment

A category-specific fixed deposit is required, alongside applicable fees and other costs.

03

Residential property

Current national categories require a property purchase at a category-specific minimum.

04

Time in Malaysia

Annual stay rules depend on age and category. Review the current guidance for your household.

05

Dependants

Family inclusion is subject to relationship, age and documentation rules.

06

Supporting documents

Prepare identity, relationship and financial evidence according to current official guidance.

Programme details may change. Verify the latest requirements with the official MM2H category overview before making a financial or application decision. Last reviewed: September 2026.
