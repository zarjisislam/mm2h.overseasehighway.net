QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

The details that matter.

A few of the questions people ask when considering Malaysia My Second Home.

Is MM2H permanent residency?+

No. MM2H is a long-term social visit pass under the applicable programme terms; it is not permanent residency or citizenship.

Can my family join me?+

The programme allows eligible dependants, subject to category rules and the current government guidelines. We can help you identify the documents required for your family.

Do I need to purchase a property?+

The current national Silver, Gold and Platinum categories include a compulsory residential property purchase, subject to category minimums and applicable rules.

Is the fixed deposit refundable?+

The official rules allow a limited withdrawal for approved purposes after approval, subject to current conditions. Confirm the precise timing and permitted use before committing funds.

Can I apply directly through Overseas Highway?+

Contact us for an initial consultation. Application submission must follow the current Malaysian government process and any licensed-agent requirements. We will explain the appropriate route for your case.

Are SEZ/SFZ requirements the same?+

No. The Special Economic Zone / Special Financial Zone route has separate age, deposit and property conditions. Ask us to review this route separately.

Programme details may change. Verify the latest requirements with the official MM2H category overview before making a financial or application decision. Last reviewed: September 2026.
