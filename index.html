THE CATEGORIES

Find your starting point.

Three national categories offer different pass terms and financial thresholds.

01 / THE CLASSIC ROUTE

Silver

Designed for those seeking a longer connection with Malaysia.

Pass term
5 years, renewable
Fixed deposit
USD 150,000
Property minimum
RM 600,000
Minimum age
25
02 / MORE TIME TO SETTLE

Gold

A longer pass term for families planning with a wider horizon.

Pass term
15 years, renewable
Fixed deposit
USD 500,000
Property minimum
RM 1,000,000
Minimum age
25
03 / THE LONG VIEW

Platinum

The longest pass term under the current national categories.

Pass term
20 years, renewable
Fixed deposit
USD 1,000,000
Property minimum
RM 2,000,000
Minimum age
25

Programme details may change. Verify the latest requirements with the official MM2H category overview before making a financial or application decision. Last reviewed: September 2026.
